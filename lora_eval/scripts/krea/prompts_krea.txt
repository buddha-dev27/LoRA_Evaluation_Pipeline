# LoRA Checkpoint Evaluation Prompts — Krea 2 Turbo
# Natural sentence-style prompts optimised for the Qwen3-VL text encoder.
# Richer photographic descriptions with lighting and scene context anchor
# Krea 2's photorealistic output. Avoid short tag-style prompts — the
# Qwen3-VL encoder needs full sentences to produce consistent results.
# All prompts produce scoreable images with visible facial information.
#
# See "Critical Eight" at the bottom for the highest-priority subset.
#
#
# Group A — Core Identity Tests (01-06)
#
# 01 - Neutral Portrait
# Baseline identity test. Failure here means the LoRA has not learned the character.
A realistic portrait photograph of a person looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression, soft frontal studio lighting, plain light grey background, upper body shot, photographic realism
# 02 - Three Quarter View
# Tests facial structure from the most common real-world angle.
A realistic portrait photograph of a person in a three quarter view, turning to look over their shoulder toward the camera, soft natural daylight, clean background, photographic realism
# 03 - Side Profile
# Tests profile accuracy — strong indicator of 3D facial structure learning.
A realistic portrait photograph showing the side profile of a person looking to the left, soft diffused natural light from the front, plain neutral background, photographic realism
# 04 - Close-Up Portrait
# Tests fine facial details at a range ArcFace can reliably detect.
A realistic close-up portrait photograph of a person looking directly at the camera, face and neck clearly visible, shoulders just entering the frame, soft frontal studio lighting, plain neutral background, photographic realism
# 05 - Hair Pulled Back
# Removes hairstyle as a crutch by exposing the full face and ears.
A realistic close-up portrait photograph with the hair pulled back behind the shoulders and ears fully visible, soft diffused natural light, plain background, photographic realism
# 06 - Directional Lighting
# Tests whether the character remains identifiable under unfamiliar lighting.
A realistic portrait photograph of a person with warm directional light from one side, soft fill light on the shadow side, three quarter view toward camera, upper body visible, plain dark background, photographic realism
#
#
# Group B — Expression Tests (07-11)
#
# 07 - Genuine Smile
A realistic portrait photograph of a person with a genuine warm smile and bright eyes, soft natural daylight, slight three quarter angle, photographic realism
# 08 - Serious Expression
A realistic portrait photograph of a person with a serious focused expression and direct steady gaze toward the camera, soft studio lighting, plain background, photographic realism
# 09 - Surprised Expression
A realistic portrait photograph of a person with a surprised expression, eyebrows raised and mouth slightly open, soft natural light, plain background, photographic realism
# 10 - Laughing
A realistic portrait photograph of a person laughing naturally with a joyful open expression and eyes crinkled with amusement, warm natural light, photographic realism
# 11 - Thoughtful Expression
A realistic portrait photograph of a person with a thoughtful contemplative expression, eyes cast slightly downward, soft diffused window light from the side, photographic realism
#
#
# Group C — Body Proportion Tests (12-16)
#
# 12 - Full Body Standing
A realistic full-body photograph of a person looking at the camera, wearing casual clothing, standing upright facing the camera, soft even studio lighting, plain background, photographic realism
# 13 - Arms Crossed
A realistic photograph of a person standing with their arms crossed, looking directly at the camera with a relaxed confident posture, soft natural lighting, photographic realism
# 14 - Hands Visible
# Hand generation is difficult. Compare across checkpoints rather than absolute score.
A realistic portrait photograph of a person facing the camera, both hands raised to shoulder height with fingers spread naturally, soft studio lighting, photographic realism
# 15 - Seated Pose
A realistic medium shot photograph of a person seated on a chair looking off camera, relaxed posture with hands resting in their lap, warm indoor lighting, photographic realism
# 16 - Cross-Legged Pose
A realistic full-body composition photograph of a person sitting cross-legged on the ground, relaxed casual posture looking at the camera, soft natural light, photographic realism
#
#
# Group D — Movement and Outfit Tests (17-20)
#
# 17 - Walking Toward Camera
A realistic full-body photograph of a person walking naturally toward the camera with eyes locked on, soft outdoor daylight, photographic realism
# 18 - Outfit Change
# Tests training data leakage. LoRAs that overfit resist outfit changes.
A realistic photograph of a person looking at the camera, wearing a plain white t-shirt and blue jeans, standing facing the camera, soft natural daylight, photographic realism
# 19 - Dynamic Action Pose
A realistic full body photograph of a person in a dynamic energetic pose with a balanced stance and arms raised, captured mid-movement, outdoor setting with natural light, photographic realism
# 20 - Dynamic Stance
# Tests upper body posture and direct engagement without hand generation issues.
A realistic upper body photograph of a person standing with arms crossed in a strong dynamic stance, looking directly at the camera with a confident expression, dramatic directional lighting, photographic realism
#
#
# Group E — Camera Angle Tests (21-24)
#
# 21 - Overhead View
A realistic portrait photograph of a person viewed from slightly above, the person looking upward toward the camera lens, soft natural overhead light, photographic realism
# 22 - Low Angle View
A realistic portrait photograph of a person viewed from below looking upward, the person gazing downward toward the camera, natural sky or ceiling in background, photographic realism
# 23 - Tight Portrait
# Tests fine facial detail while keeping the full face visible for scoring.
A realistic portrait photograph of a person from chin to forehead, looking directly at the camera, full face visible and occupying most of the frame, soft studio lighting, plain background, photographic realism
# 24 - Wide Angle Full Body
A realistic wide-angle photograph of a person standing centered in a large open environment, face visible at the top of the frame, full figure small relative to the surroundings, natural daylight, photographic realism
#
#
# Group F — Recognition Tests (25-27)
#
# 25 - Three Quarter Rear View
# Tests body orientation and partial profile while retaining enough facial
# information for meaningful CLIP scoring against the dataset.
A realistic photograph of a person in a three quarter rear view, turning to look back over their shoulder, face and eyes clearly visible near the top of the frame, natural outdoor lighting, photographic realism
# 26 - Distance Shot
# Character at small scale. Tests whether identity holds at reduced resolution.
A realistic photograph of a person standing in a large outdoor environment facing the camera, the person occupying only a small portion of the frame with surroundings dominating, natural daylight, photographic realism
# 27 - Candid Style
# Tests natural unposed appearance.
A realistic candid style photograph of a person in a natural relaxed unposed moment with a slight three quarter turn, soft outdoor overcast light, photographic realism
#
#
# Group G — Generalization Tests (28-30)
#
# 28 - Leaning Against Wall
A realistic photograph of a person leaning casually against a plain wall with arms loosely at their sides, looking at the camera, soft indoor lighting, photographic realism
# 29 - Wet or Rain Environment
# Strong generalization test — wet hair and skin significantly alter appearance.
A realistic close-up portrait photograph of a person standing outdoors in light rain with wet hair clinging to their face, overcast grey sky, photographic realism
# 30 - Complex Environment
A realistic photograph of a person standing naturally in a detailed busy outdoor urban environment with complex surroundings, full body visible, natural daylight, photographic realism
#
#
# ── Critical Eight ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# 01  Neutral Portrait       — baseline identity
# 02  Three Quarter View     — facial structure from realistic angle
# 03  Side Profile           — 3D facial structure learning
# 05  Hair Pulled Back       — identity without hairstyle crutch
# 12  Full Body Standing     — anatomy and proportion
# 14  Hands Visible          — detail retention under difficulty
# 19  Dynamic Action Pose    — identity under body deformation
# 26  Distance Shot          — recognition at reduced scale
