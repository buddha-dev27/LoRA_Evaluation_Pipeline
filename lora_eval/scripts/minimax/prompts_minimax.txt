# LoRA Checkpoint Evaluation Prompts — MiniMax H3
# Natural sentence-style prompts for the Qwen3-VL 32B text encoder.
# MiniMax H3 generates video — prompts benefit from describing the scene
# with enough context for natural motion to emerge. Keep prompts clear
# and descriptive. Guidance scale must be 1 (CFG-distilled model).
# Trigger word injected by the script — not included here.
# All prompts designed to produce a visible face for ArcFace scoring.
#
# See "Critical Eight" at the bottom for the highest-priority subset.
#
#
# Group A — Core Identity Tests (01-06)
#
# 01 - Neutral Portrait
# Baseline identity test.
A woman looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression, soft frontal studio lighting, plain light grey background, upper body shot, still and composed
# 02 - Three Quarter View
A woman in a three quarter view looking over her shoulder toward the camera, soft natural daylight, clean neutral background, composed posture
# 03 - Side Profile
A woman in side profile looking to the left, soft diffused natural light from the front, plain neutral background, still pose
# 04 - Close-Up Face
A close-up portrait of a woman with her face filling the frame, looking directly at the camera, soft frontal studio lighting, plain dark background
# 05 - Hair Pulled Back
A close-up portrait of a woman with her hair pulled back, ears fully visible, looking at the camera, soft diffused natural light, plain background
# 06 - Dramatic Lighting
A close-up portrait of a woman with dramatic side lighting casting one side of her face in deep shadow, the other side lit with warm directional light, dark background
#
#
# Group B — Expression Tests (07-11)
#
# 07 - Genuine Smile
A woman with a genuine warm smile and bright eyes, looking at the camera, soft natural daylight, slight three quarter angle
# 08 - Serious Expression
A woman with a serious focused expression and a direct steady gaze toward the camera, soft studio lighting, plain background
# 09 - Surprised Expression
A woman with a surprised expression, eyebrows raised and mouth slightly open, looking at the camera, soft natural light, plain background
# 10 - Laughing
A woman laughing naturally with a joyful open expression, looking at the camera, warm natural light
# 11 - Thoughtful Expression
A woman with a thoughtful contemplative expression, eyes cast slightly downward, soft diffused window light from the side
#
#
# Group C — Body and Movement Tests (12-16)
#
# 12 - Full Body Standing
A full body shot of a woman standing upright facing the camera, wearing casual clothing, arms relaxed at her sides, soft even studio lighting, plain background
# 13 - Arms Crossed
A woman standing with her arms crossed, looking directly at the camera with a relaxed confident posture, soft natural lighting
# 14 - Hands Visible
A woman facing the camera, both hands raised to chest height with fingers spread naturally, face clearly visible, soft studio lighting
# 15 - Seated
A woman seated on a chair facing the camera, relaxed posture with hands resting in her lap, warm indoor lighting
# 16 - Walking Toward Camera
A woman walking naturally toward the camera with a relaxed stride, face and full body visible, soft outdoor daylight
#
#
# Group D — Scene and Environment Tests (17-22)
#
# 17 - Outdoor Natural Light
A woman standing outdoors on a sunny day, looking at the camera, natural golden hour lighting, green trees and blue sky in the background
# 18 - Indoor Casual
A woman sitting casually at a wooden table in a warmly lit room, looking at the camera, bookshelves in the background, soft indoor lighting
# 19 - Urban Setting
A woman standing on a city sidewalk, looking at the camera, urban background with buildings, natural daylight
# 20 - Outfit Change
# Tests training data leakage — LoRAs that overfit resist outfit changes
A woman wearing a plain white t-shirt and blue jeans, standing and facing the camera, full body visible, soft natural daylight
# 21 - Dynamic Pose
A woman in a dynamic energetic pose with arms raised, full body visible, outdoor setting with natural light
# 22 - Distance Shot
A woman standing in a large outdoor environment facing the camera, the woman occupying only a small portion of the frame, natural daylight
#
#
# Group E — Camera Angle Tests (23-26)
#
# 23 - Overhead View
A woman viewed from slightly above, looking upward toward the camera lens, soft natural overhead light
# 24 - Low Angle View
A woman viewed from below looking upward, gazing downward toward the camera, natural background
# 25 - Three Quarter Rear
A woman in a three quarter rear view, turning to look back over her shoulder, face and eyes clearly visible, natural outdoor lighting
# 26 - Extreme Close-Up
An extreme close-up of a woman's face with the eyes filling most of the frame, looking at the camera, sharp fine detail, soft studio lighting
#
#
# Group F — Generalization Tests (27-30)
#
# 27 - Candid Style
A woman in a natural relaxed unposed moment with a slight three quarter turn, looking at the camera, soft outdoor overcast light
# 28 - Leaning Against Wall
A woman leaning casually against a plain wall, arms loosely at her sides, looking at the camera, soft indoor lighting
# 29 - Rain Environment
# Strong generalization test
A woman standing outdoors in light rain with wet hair, looking at the camera, overcast grey sky
# 30 - Leakage Check
# Control prompt — no trigger word — tests whether character bleeds into generic prompts
A woman with a neutral expression looking directly at the camera, soft studio lighting, plain background, upper body shot
#
#

# 02  Three Quarter View     — facial structure
# 03  Side Profile           — 3D facial structure
# 05  Hair Pulled Back       — identity without hairstyle
# 12  Full Body Standing     — anatomy and proportion
# 17  Outdoor Natural Light  — generalization to scene
# 21  Dynamic Pose           — identity under movement
# 22  Distance Shot          — recognition at reduced scale
