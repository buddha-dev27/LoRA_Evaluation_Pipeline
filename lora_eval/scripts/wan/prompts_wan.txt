# LoRA Checkpoint Evaluation Prompts — Wan 2.2
# Natural sentence-style prompts for the UMT5 text encoder.
# Clear, direct descriptions with explicit shot type and scene context.
# Trigger word injected by the script — not included here.
# All prompts designed to produce a single visible face for ArcFace scoring.
#
# See "Critical Eight" at the bottom for the highest-priority subset.
#
#
# Group A — Core Identity Tests (01-06)
#
# 01 - Neutral Portrait
# Baseline identity test. Failure here means the LoRA has not learned the character.
Medium shot of a woman looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression, soft frontal studio lighting, plain light grey background, upper body visible, photorealistic
# 02 - Three Quarter View
# Tests facial structure from the most common real-world angle.
Medium shot of a woman in a three quarter view turning to look over her shoulder toward the camera, soft natural daylight, clean neutral background, photorealistic
# 03 - Side Profile
# Tests profile accuracy — strong indicator of 3D facial structure learning.
Medium shot showing the side profile of a woman looking to the left, soft diffused natural light from the front, plain neutral background, photorealistic
# 04 - Close-Up Portrait
# Tests fine facial details at a range ArcFace can reliably detect.
A close-up portrait of a woman looking directly at the camera, face and neck clearly visible, shoulders just entering the frame, soft frontal studio lighting, plain neutral background, photorealistic
# 05 - Hair Pulled Back
# Removes hairstyle as a crutch by exposing the full face and ears.
Close-up portrait of a woman with her hair pulled back behind her shoulders, ears fully visible, looking at the camera, soft diffused natural light, plain background, photorealistic
# 06 - Directional Lighting
# Tests whether the character remains identifiable under unfamiliar lighting.
A portrait of a woman with warm directional light from one side, soft fill light on the shadow side, three quarter view toward camera, upper body visible, plain dark background, photorealistic
#
#

# 09 - Surprised Expression

# 10 - Laughing

# 11 - Thoughtful Expression

Medium shot of a woman standing with her arms crossed, looking directly at the camera with a relaxed confident posture, soft natural lighting, photorealistic
# 14 - Hands Visible
Medium shot of a woman facing the camera, face visible at the top of the frame, both hands raised to chest height with fingers spread naturally, soft studio lighting, photorealistic
# 15 - Seated Pose
Medium shot of a woman seated on a chair, face clearly visible, relaxed posture with hands resting in her lap, looking at the camera, warm indoor lighting, photorealistic
# 16 - Cross-Legged Pose

# Group D — Movement and Outfit Tests (17-20)
#
# 17 - Walking Toward Camera
Full body photograph of a woman walking naturally toward the camera with eyes locked on the lens, face visible at the top of the frame and feet at the bottom, soft outdoor daylight, photorealistic
# 18 - Outfit Change
# Tests training data leakage. LoRAs that overfit resist outfit changes.
Full body photograph of a woman looking at the camera, wearing a plain white t-shirt and blue jeans, standing facing the camera, head to feet visible, soft natural daylight, photorealistic
# 19 - Dynamic Action Pose
Full body photograph of a woman in a dynamic energetic pose with a balanced stance and arms raised, face visible at the top of the frame, full figure visible, outdoor setting with natural light, photorealistic
# 20 - Dynamic Stance
Medium shot of a woman standing with arms crossed in a strong confident stance, looking directly at the camera, dramatic directional lighting, photorealistic
